CONTRACT BYTECODE
=================

This is the bytecode you need to disassemble and analyze.

The bytecode contains:
- Storage operations (SSTORE/SLOAD)
- Multiple PUSH operations with embedded data
- Metadata and encrypted payload fragments

ANALYSIS:
---------
1. Disassemble: cast disassemble <BYTECODE>
2. Identify storage operations
3. Extract data from PUSH operations
4. Look for patterns in the hex data

TOOLS:
------
- cast disassemble: Bytecode disassembly
- cast storage: Storage inspection
- Python: For decryption and decoding

The encoded data is embedded in the bytecode.
You must extract and process it to recover the flag.
